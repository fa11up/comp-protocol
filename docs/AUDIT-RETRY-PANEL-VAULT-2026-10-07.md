## Resolution

All nine findings are answered in the commit after `973369e`; eight are fixed and one medium is accepted with its cost stated.

| # | Severity | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | high | **Fixed by redesign.** The aggregate lag (`laggedDebt`, `laggedSecured`, `laggedAt`, `_advanceLag`, `_clampLag`, `_approach`) is gone. Cold capital is kept per position (`Position.coldDebt`, `coldSecured`, `coldAt`); every position's cold halves every `BACKING_HALF_LIFE` (6 hours), so the vault's total cold halves at the same rate and is kept as one figure (`_coldDebt`, `_coldSecured`), read in constant time by `laggedNow()`. An increase is cold, less what the position's own bank gives back; a decrease takes the position's own cold first and banks the warm remainder on that position. No aggregate is consulted, so warmth cannot move between positions in either order. A day in which the cold total is not touched credits it in full (`_cool`), which a touch can only delay. The panel's proof passes as written (`test/retry-panel/WarmthFollowsOrdering.t.sol`, both variants). |
| 2 | medium | **Fixed** by the same redesign: a position's cold is cooled from its own date at every touch, so no stale aggregate is read. Proof passes (`test/retry-panel/StaleBank.t.sol`). |
| 3 | medium | **Fixed.** One date per bank (`bankDebtAt`, `bankSecuredAt`), set only when that bank goes from empty to full, and the expiry test runs on the side being touched whatever happens on the other. Proof passes (`test/retry-panel/BankExpiry.t.sol`). |
